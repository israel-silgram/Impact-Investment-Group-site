Communication job
By the end, prospective and existing platform partners should feel recognised, essential and confident that the new partner experience gives their role a visible place in a connected housing network.

Narrative: VIP welcome -> dedicated role experience -> shared impact chain -> architecture and QA proof.

Exactly four sections
1. VIP welcome (slides 1-2)
2. A front door for every role (slides 3-4)
3. Impact as a connected journey (slides 5-6)
4. Interactive platform architecture story (slides 7-10)

Visual direction
16:9, high-contrast navy/cream alternation, orange as decisive emphasis, teal as evidence/connection, spacious typography, real site captures, editable native diagrams.

Section 4 interaction
Presenter-led four-beat reveal: choose a partner archetype -> trace its role-specific impact logic -> reveal integration surfaces -> close with multi-device QA proof. The audience is invited to pick a role to follow.
